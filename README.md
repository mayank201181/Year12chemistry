# Year 12–13 IB Diploma Chemistry (SL & HL) Study Guide

Interactive study guide, procedural drill engine, mock-exam suite, Data Booklet reference, and Scientific Investigation (IA) toolkit for **IB Diploma Chemistry (First Assessment 2025)** — covering both DP1 and DP2 across **Structure 1–3** and **Reactivity 1–3** (`S1.1–R3.4`) with a live **SL / HL toggle**.

## Highlights
- **8 Comprehensive IB Topics (`S1.1–R3.4`):** Full SL + HL syllabus coverage with HL-only sections, questions, drills, and exams tagged `level: "HL"`.
- **FA2025 Assessment Architecture (NO Paper 3):** Mock Paper 1A (MCQ), Paper 1B (Data-Based & Experimental Skills), and Paper 2 (SL & HL structured + markband extended responses).
- **Interactive Chemistry Explorables:** Custom SVG simulators per topic (emission spectra & orbitals, VSEPR & bonding triangle, periodic trends & crystal-field colour, MS/IR/¹H NMR detective, Born–Haber & ΔG spontaneity, Maxwell–Boltzmann & Le Chatelier, pH titration curves & cells, SN1/SN2 curly-arrow stepper).
- **IA Scientific Investigation (`/ia`):** Complete 2025 4×6 criteria guide (Research Design, Data Analysis, Conclusion, Evaluation), 3,000-word budget, glassware uncertainty checklist, and interactive self-audit.
- **Zero-Login iPad Persistence:** All progress, Daily 5, SRS review, streaks, and SL/HL selection save automatically in `localStorage`.
